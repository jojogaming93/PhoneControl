THE INVESTIGATION / EVIDENCE NOTES

What the checks establish.

Private game and device screenshots are excluded from public source. The schematic atlas contains only control coordinates. Written findings retain their evidence labels.

Historical live observations established sustained camera rotation through 128 swipe restarts with another action held, followed by a new backpack action. These observations remain distinct from current automated tests.

The public classifier fixtures are generated from four anonymous arrow luminance grids on a plain background. They exercise classification, dimming, decoding, resizing and worker behavior without account UI, chat, inventory, serial numbers or device metadata. They do not establish accuracy across new gameplay scenes.
